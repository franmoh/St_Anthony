About Our Lady of Peace Parish

Three churches, one parish community on Fredericton's Northside.

Who We Are

Our Lady of Peace Parish is located on the Northside of Fredericton and serves a combination of rural and urban parishioners, including one of New Brunswick's largest First Nations communities.

Consisting of three churches — St. Anthony's, St. Patrick's and St. Theresa's — Our Lady of Peace Parish serves the spiritual, social and health needs of our community.

We welcome young families, seniors, new Canadians and visitors by offering a welcoming, inclusive community with many opportunities for spiritual growth through our many religious formation opportunities. We also provide social activities and general health and welfare services through our parish nursing program.

Our wish for our future as a parish is to subscribe to the words of John Paul II and try to live his message of Peace in our daily lives.
